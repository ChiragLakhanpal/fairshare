# Agency Incentive Agreement: Summit Travel Partners

Sample agreement written for a demo. Not a real contract. Every party, number, and term is fictional.

<!-- page 1 -->

## 1. Parties and term

This Agency Incentive Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Example Air ("Carrier") and Summit Travel Partners ("Agency"), a travel management company headquartered in Dallas, Texas. The term runs from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026 and renews for one year unless either party gives written notice sixty days before the end of the term.

## 2. Covered markets

The Agreement covers Agency's sales on the following markets in both directions: DFW-LAX, DFW-ORD, DFW-DCA, DFW-DEN, CLT-LGA, MIA-JFK, PHX-SEA, ORD-SFO, DCA-BOS, LAX-JFK, AUS-ORD, SEA-DEN (the "Covered Markets").

## 3. Definitions

"Carrier revenue share" means Agency's ticketed revenue on Carrier divided by Agency's total ticketed revenue on all carriers, measured on the Covered Markets for the period stated.

"Fair share" means Carrier's share of scheduled seats on a Covered Market in the same period, as reported to the U.S. Department of Transportation.

"Share premium" means Carrier revenue share minus fair share, in percentage points.

<!-- page 2 -->

## 4. Incentive tiers

Agency earns a back-end incentive on Carrier revenue in the Covered Markets according to its annual share premium:

| Tier | Share premium | Incentive |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | below 5 points | none |
| 2 | 5 to 9.9 points | 1.0% of Carrier revenue |
| 3 | 10 to 14.9 points | 2.0% of Carrier revenue |
| 4 | 15 points and above | 3.0% of Carrier revenue |

Incentives are calculated annually and paid within ninety days of year end.

## 5. Share maintenance

5.1 If Agency's Carrier revenue share across the Covered Markets falls below 45% for two consecutive calendar quarters, Carrier may review the Agency's tier and reduce it by one tier for the following year.

5.2 Before any reduction under 5.1, Carrier will share the quarterly share calculation with Agency and allow thirty days for Agency to dispute the figures.

5.3 A quarter in which Carrier reduced scheduled seats on a Covered Market by more than 15% year over year is excluded from the count in 5.1 for that market.

<!-- page 3 -->

## 6. Reporting

Carrier will provide Agency a monthly performance report showing Carrier revenue, Carrier revenue share, fair share, and share premium by Covered Market. Agency may request the underlying ticket-level data for its own sales once per quarter.

## 7. Goals

The parties set an annual Carrier revenue goal for 2026 of $5,184,000 and a Carrier revenue share goal of 48%. Goals are annual; there are no quarterly targets in this Agreement.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach with thirty days' written notice and a thirty-day cure period. Carrier may terminate immediately if Agency's accreditation lapses.
