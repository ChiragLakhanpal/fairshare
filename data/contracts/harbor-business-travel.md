# Agency Volume Agreement: Harbor Business Travel

Sample agreement written for a demo. Not a real contract. Every party, number, and term is fictional.

<!-- page 1 -->

## 1. Parties and term

Between Example Air ("Carrier") and Harbor Business Travel ("Agency"), a travel management company headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts. Term: January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.

## 2. Volume commitment

2.1 Agency commits to Carrier ticketed revenue of at least $4,290,000 on the Covered Markets in 2026 (the "Commitment").

2.2 Carrier pays a front-end incentive of 1.5% on all Carrier revenue in the Covered Markets, advanced quarterly against the Commitment.

2.3 If Agency's Carrier revenue at year end is below 90% of the Commitment, Agency repays the advanced incentive in proportion to the shortfall. If it is below 75%, Agency also forfeits the incentive for the first quarter of the following year.

<!-- page 2 -->

## 3. Pace reviews

Carrier and Agency meet in the first week of each quarter to review pace against the Commitment. "Pace" means year-to-date Carrier revenue divided by the year-to-date share of the Commitment, with the Commitment spread evenly across months.

## 4. Preferred carrier status

Agency designates Carrier as a preferred carrier in its booking tool for the Covered Markets and displays Carrier fares first where the fare is within $25 of the lowest available fare. Agency will notify Carrier within ten business days of any change to its preferred-carrier settings.

## 5. Reporting and data

Carrier provides a monthly performance report. Agency provides Carrier a quarterly summary of its total air spend on the Covered Markets by carrier, aggregated, for share calculation.

## 6. Termination

Thirty days' notice for material breach with a thirty-day cure period.
