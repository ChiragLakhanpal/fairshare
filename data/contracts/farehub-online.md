# Online Agency Distribution Agreement: FareHub Online

Sample agreement written for a demo. Not a real contract. Every party, number, and term is fictional.

<!-- page 1 -->

## 1. Parties and term

Between Example Air ("Carrier") and FareHub Online ("Agency"), an online travel agency headquartered in San Francisco, California. Term: January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2026.

## 2. Content and display

2.1 Carrier provides Agency its published fares and NDC content for the Covered Markets. Agency displays Carrier's branded fares with the attributes Carrier supplies (bags, seat selection, change terms) and does not relabel them.

2.2 Agency does not bias search results against Carrier. Where Agency applies a default sort, Carrier's itineraries appear in the position their price and duration earn.

## 3. Incentive

Agency earns 0.5% of Carrier revenue on the Covered Markets when its Carrier revenue share for the year is at or above fair share, and 0.25% when it is below fair share by no more than 10 points. No incentive is earned when the share gap exceeds 10 points.

<!-- page 2 -->

## 4. Data sharing

4.1 Agency shares with Carrier, monthly, aggregated search and booking data for the Covered Markets: searches, bookings, and ticketed revenue by carrier, by market, by month. No personal data is shared.

4.2 Carrier uses the data only to calculate the incentive and to review market performance with Agency. Carrier does not share Agency's data with any other agency.

4.3 Agency's monthly data is due by the tenth business day of the following month. Carrier's monthly performance report to Agency is due five business days after that.

## 5. Service levels

Carrier's NDC API is available 99.5% of the time measured monthly, excluding announced maintenance. Agency's booking confirmation is delivered to Carrier within fifteen minutes of ticketing.

## 6. Termination

Sixty days' notice for convenience by either party; thirty days for material breach with a cure period.
