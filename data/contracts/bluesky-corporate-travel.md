# Agency Incentive Agreement: BlueSky Corporate Travel

Sample agreement written for a demo. Not a real contract. Every party, number, and term is fictional.

<!-- page 1 -->

## 1. Parties and term

Between Example Air ("Carrier") and BlueSky Corporate Travel ("Agency"), a travel management company headquartered in New York, New York. Term: January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2027 (a two-year term).

## 2. Incentive

Agency earns a back-end incentive of 1.5% of Carrier revenue on the Covered Markets when its annual Carrier revenue share is at or above 43%, and 2.5% when it is at or above 47%.

## 3. Share maintenance

If Agency's Carrier revenue share falls below 40% for two consecutive calendar quarters, the parties meet within thirty days to agree a recovery plan. No tier change applies in the first year of the term.

<!-- page 2 -->

## 4. Account management

Carrier assigns a named account manager and provides a quarterly business review. Agency provides a single point of contact for performance questions and responds to Carrier's data queries within five business days.

## 5. Early termination

5.1 Either party may terminate for convenience at the end of the first contract year with ninety days' written notice.

5.2 Carrier may terminate with thirty days' notice if Agency's Carrier revenue in any two consecutive quarters is below 50% of the same quarters of the prior year, unless the decline is attributable to Carrier's own schedule reductions on the Covered Markets.

5.3 On termination, incentives accrued to the termination date are paid pro rata within ninety days.

## 6. Governing law

New York.
